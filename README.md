#  Smart Traffic Management & Emergency Transit System
>  **Regional Champion & National Finalist** — NVS National Science Exhibition (JNV Raipur)

##  Overview
An integrated smart city traffic management system designed to eliminate emergency vehicle delays, optimize energy efficiency, and prevent road damage. Features real-time density timing, wireless RF overrides for ambulances, automated street lighting, and heavy-vehicle overload sensing.

##  Key Features
- **RF Emergency Override:** Wireless RF transmitter mounted on ambulances automatically forces approaching traffic lights to signal green.
- **Smart Lighting Integration:** Automated, sensor-driven street lighting system that dynamically adjusts brightness based on ambient light levels and vehicle/  pedestrian presence to conserve municipal energy.
- **Dynamic Density Control:** Signal timing adapts to real-time traffic queues to reduce congestion.
- **Vehicle Overload Detection:** Strain/load sensors detect heavy vehicle overloads to protect road infrastructure.
- **Air Quality Telemetry:** Localized gas sensors track pollution levels at traffic intersections.

##  Hardware & Components Used
- **Microcontrollers:** Arduino Uno
- **Wireless Telecom:** 433MHz RF Transmitter & Receiver Modules
- **Sensors & Actuators:** LDR Light Sensors, Ultrasonic Modules, Strain/Load Sensors, Gas Telemetry Sensors, High-Efficiency LED Streetlights
- **Programming Language:** Embedded C / C++ (Arduino Framework)
